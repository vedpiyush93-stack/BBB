Who's who. Expert = the answer key: a person who drew a line under every line of writing and typed it. Claude = the AI being tested (it never saw the answer key). Volunteers = people on Zooniverse who drew boxes by hand. Judge = a second AI that pairs the expert's and Claude's lines by their words and says what is under every line left over; its pairs are what Found, Match and the line score count; it never changes Claude's answers.
Scores and keys
Found: of the expert's lines, how many Claude also drew. Match: of Claude's lines, how many the expert also drew.
Line score (out of 100) combines the two: high only when both are. 95+ 85+ lower
Words
On the matched lines, how many of the expert's words Claude also read.
Letters
On the matched lines, how many of the expert's letters Claude got right.
Missed and extra lines are left out of both: Found and Match already count them.
