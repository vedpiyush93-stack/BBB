Zooniverse project “Bounding Box Bonanza” · Claude, out of the box

Can Claude do the volunteers’ job?

Out of the box, with no training and no example pages, Claude is asked to find every line of handwriting on 903 manuscript pages, read its words and put a box around it: the job Zooniverse volunteers (members of the public who help researchers) do by hand. An expert who transcribed every page line by line is the answer key; the volunteers' own boxes are the comparison.

How it works

Each step is done by Claude (Claude), plain Python (Python) or Kraken, a free line-finding program that cannot read (Kraken). Claude is asked twice per page: once to read it, once to check the boxes (Claude Fable 5.2 at extra-high effort).

  1. 1
    Kraken finds the linesKraken

    For every line of writing: a line along the bottom of it, and an outline hugging it; numbered top to bottom.

    Kraken (a free, off-the-shelf line finder; it can't read)

  2. 2
    Claude reads the pageClaude

    Every line of writing: its words, which red line(s) it sits on (its own two ends where none fits); red lines with no writing are dropped.

    Claude Fable 5.2, extra-high effort: one call per page

  3. 3
    Boxes from Kraken's outlinesPython

    One box per line: the smallest rectangle around its outline, tilted with the line; stacked boxes that overlap meet halfway.

    Python: NumPy geometry (no image processing)

  4. 4
    Claude checks the boxesClaude

    A corrected box for each box that is clearly wrong (it cuts off part of its line, takes in another line, or holds no writing); then overlapping boxes meet halfway again.

    Claude Fable 5.2, extra-high effort: one more call per page

How it is scored

Evaluation only: the expert’s lines and the volunteers’ boxes never enter the method.

  1. E1
    Score against the expertPython

    Found, match, line score; words and letters right.

    Python: NumPy

  2. E2
    The judgeClaude

    Which lines pair up (by their words), and for every line left over: what is there, handwritten or printed, and a reason.

    Claude Opus 5.5, high effort

  3. E3
    Box checksPython

    Is each expert line inside one box; does each box hold one line; overlaps.

    Python: NumPy geometry

  4. E4
    The volunteersPython

    One line per box (its bottom edge), scored like Claude; best, median and consensus.

    Python: NumPy

A fair test

No answer-key data in the method

The expert’s answer key and the volunteers’ boxes are used only to score. No example pages, no learned shifts, no clean-up rules taken from them. Some prompt wording came from earlier rounds that compared Claude with the answer key (disclosed).

Checked by a poison test

The whole method is rebuilt with the answer key and the volunteers replaced by traps that crash when read; the method may read only the page images, Kraken’s lines and outlines and the saved replies. The lines and boxes come out identical on 903 of 903 pages, and all 1,806 prompts are the ones sent.

Held-out pages

Every idea was tried first on 82 tuning pages; every number above is on the other 791 pages. Five choices that looked at held-out pages (four in the method, one in the judge) are disclosed in the full write-up (to be released).
